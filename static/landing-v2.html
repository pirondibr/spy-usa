FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does WinnerTracker find competitors?

We read your site to understand the niche, then combine Google results, AI assistant suggestions and SEO data. Each company is grouped by how similar it is to you, with traffic and brand growth so you can choose fast.

Where does the data come from?

Only public sources: traffic estimates (SimilarWeb), search data (DataForSEO, Semrush), public ad libraries (Google, Meta, LinkedIn) and public profiles and posts. Estimates are labeled, and anything that looks off is flagged instead of hidden.

How does tracking work?

After your first report, turn on tracking. Every Monday at 8:00 we run the same analysis again, compare it with the last run and send only what changed: brand growth, new channels, ad pushes, viral videos and more, over 10 types of alerts. You can also run a manual check anytime. You choose the thresholds.

Which markets do you cover?

The United States and Brazil.

Do I need to connect any account?

No. There's nothing to install or connect.

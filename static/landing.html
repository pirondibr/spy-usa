FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a report take?

Finding competitors takes about a minute and a half. A full report on you and up to ten competitors usually takes a few minutes, depending on how many companies and channels you pick.

Which markets do you cover?

The United States and Brazil. Search, ads and competitor discovery are run for the market you choose.

Do I need to connect any account?

No. WinnerTracker only uses public data, so there's nothing to connect and nothing to install.

How accurate is the data?

Traffic and spend are third-party estimates — great for comparing companies, not for exact accounting. When a number looks wrong (for example, a brand name shared with an unrelated company), the report flags it instead of hiding it.

Can I add a competitor you didn't find?

Yes. On the competitor list you can add any website by hand and include it in the report.
